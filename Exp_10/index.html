Session and Cookies

Session is a server-side technology used to store user information temporarily while the user browses a website. It is commonly used for login systems, shopping carts, and user authentication.

Cookies are small text files stored in the user's browser. They are used to remember user preferences, login details, and tracking information. Cookies help websites provide a personalized experience.

Sessions are more secure because data is stored on the server, while cookies are stored on the client side.
